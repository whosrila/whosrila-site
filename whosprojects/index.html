You put months into the record. Then it drops and there is nothing to post. I shoot the reels, the photos and the video that give a release something to live on — built for artists, by one.

What I Shoot

  • Reels & Short-Form
    Vertical content cut for Instagram and TikTok — performance, studio, behind-the-scenes, teasers. Enough of them from one session to carry a release cycle instead of a single post.
  • Photography
    Press shots, cover art, promo stills. Delivered in the crops you actually need: square for DSPs, vertical for stories, wide for press.
  • Music Video
    Full visuals, performance videos and visualisers — concept through to final grade.
  • Creative Direction
    The look of the whole rollout, not just one shoot — artwork, colour, wardrobe and how the campaign hangs together.
