About the record

“Energy” exists because of a direct message. KUM3RA reached out to WHOSRILA on Instagram while his single “Gratitude” was being promoted, and the two built the song from that first contact.

Frederico Rosa produced it and Keron “Kalex” Alexander mixed and mastered it. The title came last: they didn’t settle on “Energy” until the record was finished.
