About the record

A love song written in one room by three artists. WHOSRILA, Pascal Pressure and Justice Case wrote and produced “Angels On A Sofa” together and split it evenly, three ways, which is why it is credited as a collaboration rather than a feature.

It started the night the three of them came from The Compound, the Kingston studio and event space that keeps live reggae going every Tuesday, and was finished up at Stony Hill. Pascal Pressure played the guitar, WHOSRILA cut the drums and arranged the record, and Justice Case gave it its title.

It comes from the same stretch of writing as “Made You” and carries the same warmth: Jah love, devotion, and someone who stays.

“We all sat together and wrote together, to keep everything together.”

WHOSRILA to Kaboom Magazine, September 2026
