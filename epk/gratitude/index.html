About the record

A morning prayer with a bounce. “Gratitude” starts with waking up and giving thanks to Jah, and holds on to that thanks through the rough times too: “In rough times, him hold me up like a forklift.”

Produced by Jay Mark, with a video shot by Ronaldo Russell. It was placed on Spotify’s curated new reggae playlists, and its promo run is how KUM3RA found WHOSRILA, which led to “Energy”.
