WHOSRILA is a Kingston-born artist and songwriter making conscious, self-finding music. Reggae and hip-hop are the foundation. On top of that he writes with wit, feeling and something to say.

“The man behind the music is a person of spiritual respect,” he told The Gleaner. “I focus on self-reflection, and I dwell on finding myself every day, hence the name Whosrila.” That search runs through the music. His songs deal with self-worth, spirit, resilience, love and gratitude, and they stay fun while they do it: playful lines and real emotion carry the message, so it never turns into a lecture.

His recent records are produced with UK-based CIN (Farzad Noutash) and mixed by Keron “Kalex” Alexander.

His catalogue runs from For Me in 2020 through Kingston, Protection, Outta' Line and Top Form to Made You in 2026. Gratitude and Top Form were both placed on Spotify's curated new reggae playlists, including New Reggae 2026: Fresh Export.

2026 has been his busiest year yet: Made You, his 11th single and the lead single from his upcoming EP, in August, then Energy with Kum3ra and Angels On A Sofa with Pascal Pressure and Justice Case in September. Three records in five weeks, all self-released, with no label and no one else holding the masters.

Independent. Intentional. Undeniable.
